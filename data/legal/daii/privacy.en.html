Privacy Policy

Daii · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what data Daii: Positive Affirmations ("Daii") uses, where it is stored and who receives it.

01The essentials

There is no sign-up and no account: we do not ask for your email, phone number or social media. We have no server and no user database. What you write in Daii stays on your phone.

The data controller is Ing. Dustin Díaz, reachable at info@ddagencia.com.

02What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the app:

  • The name you want us to use, if you give one (optional).
  • The themes, tone and reminder time you choose.
  • Your favorite affirmations, the affirmations you write yourself and your day streak.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widget reads today's affirmation from a shared space on the same phone. None of this travels over the internet.

03Purchases and subscription

Purchases are charged by Apple (or Google, on Android). We never see your card or payment details.

To unlock and restore Premium we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app, your Daii purchase history (which plan, when it starts, renews or ends) and technical data (model, system, language and store country). RevenueCat does not receive your name or your affirmations. Its terms are at revenuecat.com/privacy.

04Reminders

If you turn them on, Daii schedules on your phone one reminder a day at the time you choose and, during a trial, one reminder 2 days before it ends. They do not go through any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

05What we don't do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not ask for tracking permission.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for third parties' own purposes.

If you share an affirmation, the app opens your phone's share sheet and only the text you choose is sent, to whoever you choose.

06Legal basis, providers and retention

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, we process purchase data because it is needed to give you what you bought (performance of a contract).

RevenueCat, our service provider, may process it outside your country, for example in the US, with the safeguards of its contract (such as standard contractual clauses). It is kept for as long as needed to manage your purchases and for the time the law requires.

You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

07Children

Daii is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

08Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you control it: you can change it in Settings or delete it by uninstalling the app. For the purchase data RevenueCat keeps, or for any right of access, correction or deletion (including those under the GDPR and California law, CCPA/CPRA), write to info@ddagencia.com.

09Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page is updated along with the date at the top. For any questions: info@ddagencia.com

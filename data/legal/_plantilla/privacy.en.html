01The essentials

No personal data is needed to use __APP__. There is no sign-up: no email, no phone number, no social account. The app never asks for your name, your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone.

02What stays on your device

Your settings, your progress and the record of what you have bought are stored on the device itself, in the system preferences store. Uninstalling the app erases them.

03Purchases

Apple bills everything through your App Store account. The app never sees or stores your payment method, your name or your address: all it learns from the store is whether a purchase completed, so it can hand over what you bought.

04Children

__APP__ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. As no personal data is requested, there is no identifying information a child could enter through the app.

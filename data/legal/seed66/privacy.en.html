Seed66 Privacy Policy

Seed66 · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy is at https://myorange.agency/juegos/seed66/privacidad.html#en and the terms of use are at https://myorange.agency/juegos/seed66/terminos.html#en.

Seed66: 75 Day Challenge ("Seed66", "the app") is an app for tracking habit challenges. Controller: Ing. Dustin Díaz (Orange Agencia). Contact: info@ddagencia.com.

Summary

  • Your challenges, tasks, notes and photos are stored on your phone. You don't need to create an account.
  • Apple Health data never leaves your iPhone.
  • Seed66 shows no ads and does not track you.
  • We use Firebase, by Google, for usage analytics, crash reports and teams, and RevenueCat for purchases.
  • We don't sell your data.

01What stays on your phone

Your name, challenges, tasks, daily logs, notes, settings and progress photos. The camera and photo library are only used when you take or import a photo, and the poster or comparisons are only saved to your photo library if you ask. The backup in Settings > Backup and data is a file that you save or share; we never receive it. Reminders are notifications from your own phone.

02Apple Health (Premium only)

  • If you connect Apple Health, Seed66 reads, and never writes: exercise minutes, workouts, water, mindful minutes, steps and sleep. It only asks for the types your challenge's tasks use, plus exercise minutes, steps and sleep for your before and after and your numbers.
  • What for: checking off your tasks when you reach the goal, and showing your before and after, "Your challenge in numbers" and the living seed of your spiral.
  • Where: only daily totals, in an app database on your iPhone, excluded from iCloud backups. They are never sent to our servers, to Firebase, to RevenueCat or to your team, and they are not included in the backup you export. They are not used for advertising or marketing.
  • If you share a poster with your steps, you share it yourself, with the app you choose.
  • You can remove the permission in the Health app (Profile > Apps > Seed66) and delete this data in Settings > Backup and data > Delete all my data.
  • If your Premium access ends, these totals are deleted from your phone. If you come back, the app can read them from Apple Health again.

03Data that leaves your phone

3.1 Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Usage events, such as starting the onboarding, completing a day, viewing the paywall, starting a purchase or connecting Apple Health (without any health data). Also an app instance identifier and the approximate location Google infers from the IP address. It does not use the device's advertising identifier. It helps us understand how the app is used and improve it.

3.2 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Technical data about each crash (stack trace, device model, system and app version) with an installation identifier. It is used to fix bugs.

3.3 Security (Firebase App Check)

An Apple DeviceCheck token that confirms requests to teams come from the genuine app.

3.4 Teams (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore)

Only if you create or join a team:

  • An anonymous account is created: an identifier, with no email or phone number.
  • We store the team name, your display name, your color, your progress (challenge day, tasks done out of the day's tasks, streak, completed days and the status of each day) and the cheers you send. If you create the team, also the length, mode and type of challenge, without its name or its tasks.
  • Only the people on your team can see it. The names of your tasks, your notes, your photos and your health data are never shared.
  • You can leave the team and delete the account in Settings > Backup and data > Delete teams account.

3.5 Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

An anonymous RevenueCat identifier, the history of Seed66 purchases and subscriptions sent by the App Store, and technical data (model, system, language and store country). It is used to know whether you have Premium, restore your purchases and manage the trial and offers. Payment is processed by Apple: we never see your payment details.

04Legal basis (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of a contract: teams and purchases.
  • Legitimate interest: analytics and crashes, to maintain and improve the app.
  • Consent: reading Apple Health.

05Who we share it with

With Google (Firebase), RevenueCat and Apple, as service providers. They may process data outside your country, for example in the US, with the safeguards of their contracts (such as standard contractual clauses). We don't sell your data or share it for third parties' own purposes.

06How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or use "Delete all my data".
  • Teams: while you stay on the team or until you delete the teams account. A deleted team is deleted for everyone.
  • Analytics: 2 months (the retention set in Google Analytics).
  • Crashes: about 90 days.
  • Purchases: as long as the law requires.

07Your rights

  • You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data. In Mexico, the ARCO rights under the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.
  • Write to us at info@ddagencia.com. We reply within 20 business days at most.
  • In California you have the right to know what data we hold, to ask for it to be deleted and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We don't sell personal data.
  • You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.
  • What is only on your phone is under your control: "Delete all my data" deletes it.

08Children

Seed66 is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

09Changes

We will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we will let you know in the app.

Privacy Policy

Blockmix · Last updated: 23 August 2026

01The essentials

No personal data is needed to play. There is no sign-up: no email, no phone number, no social account. The game never asks for your name, your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone. The only system permission it can ask for is notifications, and only if you turn reminders on yourself.

The data controller is Ing. Dustin Díaz, reachable at info@ddagencia.com.

02What stays on your device

Your settings, your progress — scores, stages cleared, coins, unlocked items — your saved game and the record of what you have bought are all stored on the device itself, in the system preferences store.

There is no cloud backup and no account that holds them: uninstalling the app erases them.

03What is sent to a server

Only the leaderboard leaves the device, and only these three things:

  • The nickname shown on the leaderboard. It is put together from random words and you can change it whenever you like. If you type your real name into it, other players will see your real name.
  • Your best score.
  • The date and time that score was recorded.

The session is anonymous: the game signs in to Firebase with an anonymous account, an identifier with no email, no name and nothing that points back to you.

Nothing else is uploaded. Not your coins, not your stages, not your purchases, not any device identifier.

04Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. The ads are not personalised: the app never asks for tracking permission and never uses your advertising identifier to profile you or follow you across other apps.

To serve an ad, measure whether it was seen and detect fraud, Google's SDK does collect technical data about the device. That processing is governed by Google's own policies, not by this one.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, a consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can reopen it whenever you like from Settings, under Privacy options.

Buying Remove ads, or either of the two annual subscriptions, takes the advertising away — and with it, everything described in this section.

05Purchases and subscriptions

The game sells one-off items — coin packs, the piggy bank, the welcome pack and Remove ads — and two auto-renewing annual subscriptions: Blockmix No Ads and Blockmix VIP.

Apple bills all of it through your App Store account. The app never sees your payment method, your name or your address. All it learns from the store is whether a purchase completed and, for each subscription, the date it is paid up to, so that it can hand over what you bought.

Which of the two plans you are on and when your period ends are kept on the device itself. They are not uploaded anywhere and they are not attached to your leaderboard nickname.

06Notifications

Reminders are created on your own device, never sent from a server, and only if you turn them on. Switching them off in the app's settings or in the system settings also cancels the ones already scheduled.

07Children

Blockmix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. As no personal data is requested, there is no identifying information a child could enter through the app.

08Your rights

Uninstalling the app erases everything kept on the device.

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, write to info@ddagencia.com quoting the nickname you used. You may also ask for a copy of what is held about you: it will be exactly the three fields listed in section 3.

If you live in the European Union you can exercise the rights of access, rectification, portability, restriction and erasure granted to you by the GDPR at that same address. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

09Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the text inside the app and the copy published on the web are updated together, along with the date at the top.

For any question: info@ddagencia.com

Terms of Use and EULA

Blockmix · Last updated: 23 August 2026

01What Blockmix is

Three pieces per round: fit them into the grid and clear lines before you run out of room, across its block, hex and sliding boards. The app is developed by Ing. Dustin Díaz and distributed through the App Store for iPhone.

By downloading and using Blockmix you accept these terms. If you do not agree with them, do not use the app.

02Licence

You are granted a personal, non-exclusive, revocable and non-transferable licence to use the app on devices that belong to you. You may not resell it, rent it, decompile it or redistribute it.

03Coins and in-game items

Coins, skins, boards and every other unlockable item have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, cannot be transferred to another player and do not leave the app. They are game content and may be rebalanced or changed in future versions.

Purchased coins do not expire. There is no cloud backup: if you uninstall the app, your progress and your items cannot be recovered.

There is no gambling in the app: no entry fees, no wagering and no cash prizes.

04One-off purchases

These are bought once and nothing renews:

  • Consumable — coin packs and the piggy bank. They are spent in the game and can be bought again.
  • Non-consumable — the welcome pack and Remove ads. They are bought once and stay yours. If you change device or reinstall, the Restore purchases button in the shop brings them back.

Remove ads does that and nothing else: it takes the advertising away for good, once, with nothing renewing. It gives no coins, no lives and no skins. It is not the same thing as Blockmix No Ads, which is one of the two annual subscriptions in the next section.

The price you see is the one the App Store sets in your own currency, and Apple handles the billing.

05The two annual subscriptions

Blockmix sells two auto-renewing annual subscriptions. They differ only in what they unlock:

  • Blockmix No Ads — no advertising, 200 coins a day, three daily spins on the wheel and double the piggy bank.
  • Blockmix VIP — everything above, with 500 coins a day instead of 200, plus unlimited lives and every skin unlocked.

VIP deliberately includes everything the cheaper plan has: it costs more, and there would be no way to explain someone paying more and getting less.

Everything else applies to both alike:

  • Length: one year per period.
  • Price: the one App Store shows in your own currency next to the purchase button, before you confirm anything. Apple charges it to your App Store account when you confirm.
  • Automatic renewal: it renews for another year at the price then in force, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • How to cancel: from your App Store account, under Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not cut short the period you already paid for: what you bought runs to the end of it.
  • There is no free trial and no introductory offer.
  • If you change device or reinstall, Restore purchases in the shop brings back a subscription that is still active.

06Refunds and right of withdrawal

Refunds are governed by Apple's policy. The developer cannot process refunds directly; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Right of withdrawal (Directive 2011/83/EU): for digital content bought through the App Store, consumers in the European Union have 14 days to withdraw, handled through Apple's official refund channels.

07Advertising

The free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob. The developer does not choose the content of each ad and does not endorse the products advertised. Buying Remove ads takes the advertising away for good, and either of the two annual subscriptions takes it away while it stays active.

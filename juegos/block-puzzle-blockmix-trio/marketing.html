Block Puzzle: Blockmix Trio

Blockmix Trio is a block puzzle: every round hands you three pieces to fit into the grid. Each row or column you complete clears out and gives the space back. There is no clock and no countdown — the game ends when none of the three pieces fits any more, so every placement is really a decision about the gap you leave for the next one.
